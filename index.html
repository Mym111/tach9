Station log

Tach 9

Push your station past the speed of light, one jump at a time.

How to play
1
Match in engineering

Tap a tile, then any tile in the same row or column. The swap goes through if it makes a match of 3 or more.

Power routing

2
Watch the viewport

Contacts fly toward you. Each badge shows the system and charge it needs. Have that charge ready before it reaches you.

3
Stay alive

Hull is the gauge left of the board, fuel is on the right. The pursuer's gap is top-left of the viewport. Misses close it.

4
Chain for perks

Cascades of ×3, ×4 and ×5+ fire the perks you equip in the War room's combo loadout.

5
Reach Tach 9

Clear planets for credits, research one project at each, and recover gate parts. Then attempt the next Tach at the Exodus Gate: five reels, one per system, and all five must hold.

5
Reach the gate

Clear the system, bank Credits, and spend them on upgrades. A loss keeps what you collected.
